  "Tail pinned" holds the ability process at its benchmark value and
  recalibrates only theta and beta. "Tail diagnostic" begins from the

  refitting any moment; its own moments therefore drift, and it is a
  diagnostic rather than a calibrated column. Misallocation reduced is
  1 - M_benchmark/M_j; entry repaired is 1 - S_benchmark/S_j.

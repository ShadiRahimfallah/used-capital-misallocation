--- 1. STATED REASON FOR NOT APPLYING FOR A FORMAL LOAN ----------------
  (q87_11, q87_13, q77_15; firms that did not apply since the last round)

  reason                            2011      2013      2015    pooled
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Inadequate collateral             3.1%      2.7%      2.2%      2.6%
  Don't want to incur debt         14.6%     17.4%     23.1%     18.5%
  Process too difficult             8.3%     10.1%      6.8%      8.4%
  Didn't need one                  57.3%     50.6%     54.5%     54.1%
  Interest rate too high           14.4%     14.5%      6.9%     11.8%
  Already heavily indebted          0.9%      1.6%      0.7%      1.0%
  Other                             1.5%      3.1%      5.9%      3.6%
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  N answering                      1,743     1,882     1,993     5,618

  READING.  Inadequate collateral is 2.6% of stated reasons pooled
  (147 firms of 5618).  The modal answer is 'didn't need one'.  The draft's
  sentence -- 'a large share of those that never applied ... lacked
  collateral' -- is NOT supported and must be rewritten.  What the data
  do support: most non-applicants are self-financing by choice or price
  (no need, unwilling to take on debt, rate too high), and a further
  9.4% are turned away by the process or by existing debt.
